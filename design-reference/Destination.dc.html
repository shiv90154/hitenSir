Manali

Kullu District, Himachal Pradesh, India

About Manali

Nestled in the Kullu Valley on the banks of the Beas River, Manali is Himachal Pradesh's most-loved hill town — a gateway to snow-capped peaks, alpine meadows and adventure sports, framed by deodar forests on every side.
